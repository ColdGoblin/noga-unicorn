# Picture list: Noga and the Unicorn

Version 1, 14 Nov 2026. For Ghost. Do not start this list until the owner approves the style sheet. Where each picture is used: LEVEL-DESIGNS.md.

## Rules for every picture

- **One style:** soft pastel storybook, thick clean outlines, flat soft shading, matching the story videos and the style sheet the owner approves.
- **Characters and items:** PNG with a transparent background, trimmed close to the drawing, facing right, whole body visible.
- **Backgrounds:** JPG, no characters and no text in them.
- **No writing** inside any picture (the game has no reading).
- **Names:** exactly as listed, lower case. Deliver into `noga-unicorn\art\` in the folders named below.
- **Sizes** are the sizes to deliver. Hex shrinks them for the game.
- **Free tools only.**
- **Noga always:** five years old, blue eyes, light-brown wavy hair, fair skin, small gold crown, teal sweatshirt with a heart, pink trousers.
- **The unicorn always:** small foal, marshmallow white, pastel rainbow mane and tail, golden horn, no wings.
- **Take each friend's look from the story plates** in `G:\My Drive\Projects\noga stories`. If a friend has no picture there, say so in your delivery note instead of inventing silently.
