# Voice script: Noga and the Unicorn

110 clips. Version 1, 14 Nov 2026. Do not record the full set until the owner and Noga approve the five-line sample.

## Direction

- **Narrator (N):** one warm, bright, full-voiced woman, like a kindergarten teacher speaking across a sunny room. Clear, a little slower than normal speech, smiling. Never whispery, breathy, sleepy or close to the microphone.
- **Listener:** a five-year-old Hebrew speaker who is learning these words. Say every word fully. Leave a small pause between sentences.
- **Name:** Noga is pronounced NO-gah (stress on NO, hard g).
- **Friends:** each friend line has its own character voice (noted in the table). If separate voices are not possible, the narrator performs them playfully.
- **Fixed wording:** record the text exactly. The game teaches by repeating the same words, so "Jump!" must never become "Hop!".

## Files

- One clip per file, named by its id: `ins_01.mp3`, `num_03.mp3`, and so on.
- mp3, mono, 44.1 kHz, about 96 kbps. Trim silence to about 0.1 s at each end.
- Same loudness across all clips (about -17 LUFS, as in the story videos).
- Deliver into `noga-unicorn\voice\full\`.
